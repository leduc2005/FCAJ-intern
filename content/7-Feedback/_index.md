Below are my personal reflections and feedback following the First Cloud AI Journey internship program, hoping to offer insights to further improve future cohorts.

**1. Learning and Working Environment**  
I was highly impressed by the program's openness and practical approach. Giving the team full autonomy—from designing the architecture and selecting services to managing cost limits—felt like working in a real tech startup rather than just completing academic lab exercises.

**2. Support from Mentors and Team Admins**  
The mentors were always attentive and provided sharp guidance, especially when our team was debating infrastructure optimization. Their method of asking thought-provoking questions to help us identify integration errors on our own, instead of spoon-feeding solutions, greatly sharpened my troubleshooting skills.

**3. Alignment with Career Goals**  
The training roadmap, which transitioned from core foundations (IAM, EC2, VPC) to modern Serverless and AI technologies, perfectly aligned with my goal of becoming a Cloud Engineer. Building and connecting various resources firsthand gave me a profound understanding of the big picture.

**4. Opportunities for Skill Development**  
Beyond technical configuration and deployment skills on AWS, I learned how to manage projects, collaborate remotely, and author professional technical documentation. Furthermore, controlling costs (Billing & Budgets) has now become a natural reflex whenever I design any cloud architecture.

**5. Suggestions for Improvement**  
I have a few minor suggestions: (1) Provide more documentation or a dedicated session on account setup and permission management using AWS Organizations, as multi-member teams risk security issues if sharing a single root account; (2) Offer basic templates (like Terraform or CloudFormation) so teams can learn and reference Infrastructure as Code (IaC) deployments more quickly.

**6. Post-Submission Optimization Phase (01/08 – 14/08/2026)**  
Although the final report was submitted on 31/07/2026, the university's official internship period extended until 14/08/2026. Our team utilized this window to further refine the system:
* **Infrastructure Optimization:** Fine-tuned the memory allocation for Lambda functions to find the sweet spot between performance (reducing cold starts) and cost.
* **API Security:** Implemented Authentication and Rate Limiting on API Gateway to prevent DDoS risks and AI API abuse.
* **Process Documentation:** Finalized a detailed deployment runbook, ensuring that the entire system could be quickly and accurately reproduced by any engineer from an empty AWS account.

**7. Program Recommendation**  
I will absolutely recommend this program to my juniors. It is a rare playground that offers a comprehensive and professional experience in the entire cloud product development lifecycle at near-zero personal cost.
