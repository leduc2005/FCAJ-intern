This worklog records the 7-week internship as part of the **First Cloud AI Journey** programme, running from **15/06/2026 to 31/07/2026**.

The first five weeks were dedicated to a self-paced sprint through the [AWS Cloud Journey](https://cloudjourney.awsstudygroup.com/) curriculum (296 videos) combined with intensive hands-on labs on the AWS Console. Each week focused on a core service domain (Foundations, Compute, Storage, Database, Serverless, etc.) to build a strong theoretical and practical understanding.

The final two weeks (Weeks 6 and 7) marked the intensive teamwork phase, focusing on ideation, writing the proposal, and fully executing the **Toxic Text Moderation Platform** project. During this capstone phase, my primary responsibilities included setting up the multi-account structure (AWS Organization) and building the serverless Backend infrastructure (Lambda, DynamoDB), working closely with the team to meet the 31/07/2026 submission deadline. This timeline perfectly aligns with the HCMUT external internship (TTNT, HK253) registered for **15/06/2026 – 14/08/2026** (Form D2).
